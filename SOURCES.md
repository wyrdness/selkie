## Ballads & Poetry

### Traditional Ballads
- "The Great Silkie of Sule Skerry" — Traditional Scottish/Orcadian ballad (Child Ballad #113)
  - Variants documented by Francis James Child (1882-1898)
  - Recorded by multiple artists including Joan Baez, Steeleye Span, Sting
- "The Grey Selchie" — Shetland variant
- "The Maid and the Selkie" — Orkney traditional

### Poetry
